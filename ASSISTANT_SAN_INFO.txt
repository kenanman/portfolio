1. WHAT IS ASSISTANT-SAN?
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ASSISTANT-SAN is a standalone desktop AI companion robot powered by an ESP32-S3
microcontroller. It connects directly to Wi-Fi and streams bidirectional,
real-time voice audio to Google's Gemini Multimodal Live API via a lightweight
cloud relay (no local PC required to operate).

Key Features:
- Real-Time Voice Conversation: Full-duplex speech-to-speech with Google Gemini.
- mmWave Radar Spatial Tracking: A 24GHz radar tracks human coordinates and
  distance in real time; the assistant's digital eyes track the user's position
  across the desk and automatically wake up within a 1.5-meter radius.
- Animated Vector Display: A 2.8" color TFT screen shows expressive, reactive eyes
  (Idle, Listening, Thinking, Speaking) plus HUD data (temperature, humidity, distance).
- 360° ARGB Halo Underglow: 24-LED addressable RGB ring shines onto the desk with
  breathing cyan, pulsing green, and voice-reactive blue/purple animations.
- Ambient Telemetry: Built-in DHT22 sensor injects room temperature and humidity
  directly into Gemini's system instructions so it knows its physical environment.
- High-Power Sound: Built-in 5W X-Rocker speaker driver inside an acoustic chamber.


2. COMPLETE HARDWARE MANIFEST & SPECIFICATIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Microcontroller:
   - ESP32-S3-WROOM-1-N16R8 (16MB Flash, 8MB Octal OPI PSRAM).
   - CRITICAL CONSTRAINT: GPIO 33, 34, 35, 36, and 37 are internally reserved for
     Octal PSRAM and CANNOT be used. GPIO 26-32 reserved for Flash.
     Strapping pins (0, 3, 45, 46) and native USB (19, 20) are protected.

2. Audio Input (Microphone):
   - InvenSense ICS-43434 digital I2S MEMS microphone breakout.
   - 16-bit mono 16kHz audio capture via DMA.

3. Audio Output (Amplifier & Speaker):
   - MAX98357A I2S Class-D mono amplifier breakout (5W).
   - X-Rocker 4 Ohm, 5W speaker driver unit (83.0 mm x 83.0 mm x 50.0 mm).

4. Display:
   - 2.8-inch ILI9341 SPI TFT LCD (240x320 resolution).
   - PCB dimensions: 86.0 mm x 50.0 mm. Active window: 57.6 mm x 43.2 mm.

5. Spatial Tracking Radar:
   - Hi-Link HLK-LD2450 24GHz multi-target human tracking mmWave radar.
   - Interface: UART @ 256,000 baud (extracts X/Y coordinates and distance).
   - PCB dimensions: 44.0 mm x 15.0 mm.

6. Underglow Lighting:
   - 24-Bit WS2812B NeoPixel ARGB LED ring.
   - Dimensions: 66.0 mm Outer Diameter (OD), 52.0 mm Inner Diameter (ID).

7. Environmental Sensor:
   - DHT22 (AM2302) temperature & humidity digital sensor (1-Wire).

8. Power Supply:
   - Standard 5V @ 3.0A (15W) USB-C power brick.

4. POWER ARCHITECTURE (4 ISOLATED DOMAINS)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
To prevent speaker amplifier current spikes and LED switching noise from buzzing
into the microphone line or causing radar ghost targets:

1. 5V_DIRTY (High-Current Pulsed Domain):
   - Powers: MAX98357A 5W Amp + 24-LED WS2812B ARGB ring.
   - Filter: 1000 uF low-ESR bulk electrolytic capacitor + 100 nF ceramic.
   - Draw: ~470 mA nominal, up to 1.75 A peak burst.

2. 5V_RADAR (RF Filtered Domain):
   - Powers: HLK-LD2450 24GHz mmWave radar.
   - Filter: 600 ohm @ 100MHz Ferrite bead + 22 uF + 100 nF Pi-filter.
   - Draw: ~120 mA average.

3. 3V3_DIGITAL (Logic & Display Domain):
   - Powers: ESP32-S3 SoC + ILI9341 2.8" TFT display + DHT22.
   - Draw: ~250 mA nominal (up to 450 mA during Wi-Fi transmission).

4. 3V3_ANALOG_MIC (Ultra-Clean Audio Domain):
   - Powers: ICS-43434 digital MEMS microphone only.
   - Regulator: Dedicated high-PSRR 3.3V LDO (e.g. TLV75533 / LP5907) with 10 uF
     and 100 nF decoupling right at the mic pins (< -88 dBFS noise floor).
   - Draw: ~1.5 mA.

Total Power: ~0.85 A (4.25 W) nominal conversation | ~2.38 A (11.9 W) max peak.
Powered by a single 5V 3A USB-C connection with star grounding.


5. SOFTWARE & CLOUD ARCHITECTURE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A. ESP32-S3 Firmware (ASSISTANT_SAN.ino) — Dual-Core FreeRTOS:
   - Core 0 (Network & Audio Pipeline):
     * Maintains persistent WebSocket stream to Cloud Relay.
     * Captures 16kHz 16-bit mono PCM from ICS-43434 via DMA into PSRAM ring buffer.
     * Receives 24kHz/16kHz PCM audio chunks from Cloud Relay into playback ring
       buffer feeding MAX98357A I2S DAC.
     * Low-latency barge-in support: instantly flushes audio queue when user speaks.
   - Core 1 (UI, Radar & Lighting):
     * Parses 30-byte binary UART frames from HLK-LD2450 at 256,000 baud.
     * Real-time eye tracking: moves eye pupils on TFT based on user's X coordinate.
     * Proactive wake: automatically activates screen/session when user is within 1.5m.
     * 35+ FPS eye animations: Idle (blinking), Listening (wide pupils), Thinking, Speaking.
     * 24-LED ARGB halo: Breathing cyan (idle), pulsing green (listening), voice-reactive.
     * DHT22 background polling every 2 seconds.

B. Zero-PC Cloud Relay (main.py, Dockerfile):
   - Built with Python FastAPI & official google-genai SDK.
   - Containerized for 1-click deployment on Google Cloud Run or Render.
   - Handles Gemini Live WebSocket handshake (`BidiGenerateContent`).
   - Resamples Gemini's 24kHz audio to 16kHz via polyphase filter for ESP32.
   - Injects real-time spatial (radar distance) and environmental (DHT22 temp/humidity)
     context into Gemini's system instructions seamlessly.


6. ENCLOSURE & 3D PRINTING DESIGN (assistant_san_case.scad)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Printer Profile: Voxelab Aquila (220 x 220 x 250 mm bed), 0.4 mm nozzle.
- Filament: White PLA (thin 0.8mm walls act as translucent ARGB diffuser,
  thin 1.2mm walls act as 24GHz RF-transparent radome for the radar).
- Build Plate: Textured PEI plate (Front Bezel prints face-down for matte finish).
- 3-Piece Modular Enclosure:
  1. Main_Body: Acoustic chamber for 83x83x50mm X-Rocker speaker, electronics bay,
     hexagonal acoustic grille, rear USB-C cutout, and thermally isolated DHT22 vent.
  2. Front_Bezel: Recessed visor for 2.8" TFT, stealth radome pocket for radar,
     and isolated pinhole microphone acoustic port with silicone gasket seat.
  3. Bottom_Diffuser_Base: Circular channel for 24-LED ring with elevated feet
     casting 360-degree underglow onto the desk.
- Design Rules: All overhangs <= 45 degrees for 100% zero-support printing.
